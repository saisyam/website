## The idea

Plenty of people have stories to tell but can't draw. This project is a comic-style image generator built for creators: design a character once, keep it consistent across panels, and lay out pages for a comic.

## Approach

It's built on open image models and focused only on cartoon and comic styles, not on photorealism.
